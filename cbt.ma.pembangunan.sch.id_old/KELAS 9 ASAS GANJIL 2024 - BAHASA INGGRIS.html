How to Make Cheesecake

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup of sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon of vanilla
  • 2 packages of cream cheese

Directions:

  • Beat and blend sugar, cream cheese, and vanilla at medium speed.
  • Blend in the eggs, then stir.
  • Bake at 35 celsius for 40 minutes or until it is almost set.
  • Cool.
  • To get the best result, put it in the refrigerator for 3 hours.

How to make an Omelette

 

(1) Pour the egg mixture into the centre of the pan and cook over a high heat for 1-2 minutes

(2) Warm a medium frying pan over a high heat and a dash of oil.

(3) Gently beat the egg together with salt, pepper, and a teaspoon of cold water.

(4) As the egg begins to set, use a spatula to push the set egg towards the omelette centre.

(5) Cook the set omelette for another minute, then loosen the edges with a spatula and fold the omelette in half.

(6) Tilt the pan and slide the omelette onto a warm plate and serve immediately on its own or with a crisp green salad.

(7) Continue this action until the entire egg mixture is set.
